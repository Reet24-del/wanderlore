# Optional demo recording

The [published DEV article](../submission/DEV-POST.md) uses screenshots and a deployed preview; no video was required for publication.

For an additional recording, open the HTTPS preview or start the local server. Keep the tab visible. Use Sample walk on a laptop and clearly say its route is simulated. Use your phone for real location; the owner reported phone GPS working while the Mac browser timed out.

1. Show the illustrated landing and explain that Wanderlore turns a walk into an interactive audio story.
2. Choose a genre and weather. Rainy adds drizzle and ambience after sound is enabled.
3. Begin the sample walk and let narration play. Available narration defaults are selected by the app; engine and voice selectors are no longer on the landing form.
4. Choose a story action and show its influence in the next scene. Alternatively enable Continue automatically, leave narration to end, and keep the quiet eight-second pause visible.
5. Pause/resume, request a closing chapter, Finish & save, add a reflection, and show the Journal.

Do not show API keys, pairing codes, private account settings, or real GPS exports. Never present a sample route as a real walk. Label generation edits honestly. Hosted narration uses internet; offline narration refers to local downloaded models with the local server running. Background/locked-phone operation is not implemented.
