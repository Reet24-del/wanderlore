# Wanderlore sponsor strategy — 10 October 2026

Sources: [official Week 1 challenge](https://dev.to/challenges/hacktoberfest-week1-2026-10-05), [challenge hub](https://dev.to/challenges/hf26), and the user's screenshot of apparent category claims. Screenshot counts are not verified eligible entries or win probabilities. They may overlap, omit unpublished projects, and change before judging.

The official pages permit multiple genuinely used partner categories, with one win per submission. Writing is the most heavily weighted criterion. Week 1 closes October 11 at 11:59 PM PDT, corresponding to October 12 at 12:29 PM IST. Outdoor evidence and a clear write-up remain priorities.

## Recommended work order (proposal, not completed integrations)

1. **DigitalOcean (4 screenshot claims):** host the functional HTTPS demo and model runtime. First target because deployment solves a real phone-access problem. Requires account, budget, adequate model memory and CPU, TLS, authentication and explicit cloud privacy copy. The local edition remains usable.
2. **Tiger Data (5):** an optional library of public Lucknow lore with hybrid retrieval and sources, used to prepare downloadable story packs. Avoid uploading personal route history. Requires a real retrieval demonstration; merely adding a dependency is insufficient.
3. **Entire (7):** record actual future development sessions and use those records to explain decisions in the write-up. Verify supported coding client first. Do not retroactively claim old sessions were recorded with Entire.
4. **Sentry Agent Tracing (11):** instrument chapter generation and voice latency, document a genuine failure and fix. Exclude GPS, story text, prompts and personal data from exported telemetry by default.
5. **ElevenLabs (27):** optional connected narration or generated demo narration, addressing the user's voice-quality concern. Keep local Kokoro available; label cloud narration accurately.
6. **Gemma (194):** already central to the local narrator. Strengthen evidence with coherent sample stories and honest latency measurements rather than replacing it.

Then consider **Temporal (8)** for durable chapter/audio jobs if deployment testing shows recovery gaps; **Backboard (9)** for a documented open-model comparison; **SerpApi (13)** for sourced public research during map-pack preparation. Each needs working evidence and available time.

## Current reality

Only Gemma is already demonstrated in the app among these partners. Hosting, cloud retrieval, traces, session capture and ElevenLabs remain proposed. No sponsor account was created, cloud resource provisioned, key requested in chat, or user data sent to a sponsor during this assessment.

## Location issue

The former UI merged position-unavailable and timeout into one message. These are now separated from permission denial. A failed high-accuracy request gets one ordinary-accuracy attempt; a real fix must still meet the existing 80 m threshold. HTTPS/localhost and provider availability remain required. The offline Lucknow pack cannot substitute for a real position. No live location was collected or verified during this fix.
